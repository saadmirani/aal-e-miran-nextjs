## About the Family

**Saadaat-e-Kharbaiyya** traces its lineage to **Syedna Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib (A.S.)**, the fourth Caliph of Islam. The family descends from a distinguished lineage of scholars and Sufi saints who played a significant role in the spiritual and religious history of Bihar.

The most prominent ancestor of this family is **Hazrat Makhdoom Syed Waheed-ud-din Chillakash Mashhadi Rizwi (R.H.)**, who migrated from **Mashhad, Iran**, and settled in **Bihar Sharif** or its surrounding region. He was married to the sister of **Hazrat Khwaja Najeebuddin Firdausi (R.A.)**, named **Bibi Barkah (R.H.)**, the daughter of **Hazrat Makhdoom Syed Zaki-ud-din (R.A.)** and granddaughter of the celebrated Sufi saint **Hazrat Makhdoom-e-Jahan Sheikh Sharfuddin Ahmad Yahya Maneri (R.H.)**. Through these noble lineages, his descendants established themselves as one of the most respected Saadaat families of Bihar and eventually spread across several nearby settlements, including **Sonera, Palasi, Badar Adaad, Makhdoompur**, and **Bihar Sharif**.

Among his distinguished descendants was **Miran Syed Habib (R.H.)**, who married **Syeda Bibi Maalo (R.H.)** Daughter of **Miran Syed Shah Muhammad**, popularly known as **Bibi Maleeha (R.H.)**, a noble lady from **Miran Bigha, Tekari**. According to the multiple sources, she purchased the village of **Kharbaiyya** during a public auction by offering her gold bangles as payment. A detailed account of this remarkable event is preserved in her biography. Following the acquisition of the village, the family permanently settled there, making Kharbaiyya one of their principal ancestral homes.

**Bibi Maleeha (R.A.)** was laid to rest in **Kharbaiyya**, where her shrine continues to exist to this day. It remains a place of deep reverence not only for her descendants but also for the local non-Muslim community. Although the Muslim families of the village migrated after the communal disturbances of **1946**, her shrine continues to be preserved and respected, serving as a lasting symbol of the family's spiritual legacy.
