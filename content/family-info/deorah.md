## About the Family

**Saadaat-e-Dewra, Osas** traces its lineage to **Syedna Hazrat Uthman ibn Affan (R.A.)**, the third Caliph of Islam. The family descends from a distinguished lineage of scholars and Sufi saints who played a significant role in the spiritual and religious history of Bihar.

According to family tradition, one of their revered ancestors, **Hazrat Makhdoom Shah Burhanuddin (R.H.)**, popularly known as **Khoond Miyan Dewrawi**, first arrived in **Baiganabad, Bihar**, before eventually settling in **Osas Dewra, Gaya**. The families of **Simla, Dewra, Makaram Chak, and Saadipur** all trace their ancestry to the eminent Sufi saint **Hazrat Khwaja Muhammad Jalaluddin Kabir-ul-Auliya (R.H.) of Panipat**.

Over the centuries, **Saadaat-e-Dewra** has earned recognition as one of the prominent Saadaat families of Bihar, with matrimonial and spiritual ties extending across numerous Syed families in the region.

One of the family's greatest spiritual distinctions is the custodianship of the **Ziyarat-e-Rasool ﷺ**, a revered relic that is traditionally displayed to devotees during the annual **Urs** celebrations. The shrine complex at **Osas, Dewra** remains an important centre of spiritual devotion, where many of the family's Sufi saints are buried and continue to attract visitors from across the country.
