## About the Family

**Saadaat-e-Miran Bigha, Tekari** belongs to the **Qadri (Qadiriyya) Sufi Order** and traces its lineage back to the **Sayedna Shaikh Mohi-uddin Abdul Qadir Jilani R.H** and Sufi saints who migrated from **Baghdad** to the Indian subcontinent.

According to the available sources, **Miran Syed Shah Muhammad Qadri** (or, according to some accounts, his son **Miran Syed Raaje Manikpuri (R.A.)**) migrated from **Baghdad** and settled in **Manikpur, Allahabad (Prayagraj)**. From this lineage came **Miran Syed Shah Ahmad Qadri**, who later migrated from Manikpur and settled in **Mira Peth, Jehanabad**, where he spent the rest of his life and was laid to rest.

His son, **Miran Syed Qutub Alam**, popularly known as **Miran Bhik**, migrated from Mira Peth and established a new settlement around **800 AH**. The village was originally known as **Saidpur, Bara**, but due to the profound spiritual influence and respect commanded by Miran Syed Qutub Alam, it gradually came to be known as **Miran Bigha r Mira Bigha**, a name by which it is still recognized today.

At present, the majority of the descendants of **Miran Syed Qutub Alam (Miran Bhik)** reside in **Miran Bigha, Tekari**, and many members of the family are also buried there, making the village an important ancestral and spiritual centre of the Saadaat family.
