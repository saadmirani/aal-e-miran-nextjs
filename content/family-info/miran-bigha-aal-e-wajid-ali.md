## About the Family

**Saadaat-e-Miran Bigha (Wajidi Branch)** is one of the two prominent Saadaat families of **Miran Bigha, Tekari**. This branch traces its lineage to **Hazrat Makhdoom Syed Waheed-ud-din Chillakash Mashhadi Rizwi (R.A.)** and is therefore part of the distinguished Hussaini lineage descending from **Syedna Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib (A.S.)**.

The family's principal ancestor in Miran Bigha was **Hazrat Syed Wajid Ali (R.H.)**, whose forefathers originally resided in and around **Bihar Sharif**. Following a period of communal unrest, the family migrated to **Miran Bigha, Tekari**, where they permanently settled alongside the descendants of **Hazrat Miran Syed Qutub Alam, popularly known as Miran Bhik (R.A.)**.

Among the most distinguished members of this branch was **Hazrat Syed Aal-e-Nabi (R.H.)**, who earned widespread respect for his social, religious, and cultural contributions to the community. A detailed account of his life and achievements is available in the **Biography** section.

Over time, the descendants of **Hazrat Syed Wajid Ali (R.A.)** established close matrimonial ties with the family of **Hazrat Miran Syed Qutub Alam (R.A.)**, further strengthening the relationship between the two branches of Saadaat in Miran Bigha. Today, many members of this family continue to reside in the village, while several generations of their ancestors are buried there, preserving their enduring historical and spiritual legacy.
