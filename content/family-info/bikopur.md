## About the Family

**Saadaat-e-Bikopur** belongs to the **Hussaini** lineage, tracing its ancestry to **Syedna Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib (A.S.)** and the noble family of the **Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.**

The family has historical connections with several prominent Saadaat settlements, including **Kharbiya, Bihar Sharif, Kopa, Palasi, Ahmadpur Jhikatiya**, and other branches spread across Bihar.

According to family tradition, **Hazrat Syed Rajab Ali (R.H.)** migrated from **Samad Chak, Rafiganj (Aurangabad)**, the ancestral homeland of **Saadaat-e-Ahmadpur** and eventually settled in **Bikopur**. He was a respected **Zamindar** and was well known for his **generosity**, having donated considerable portions of his land for charitable purposes.

Although the exact period and circumstances of his migration to Bikopur remain uncertain, ongoing research aims to document his life and contributions more comprehensively. Today, his descendants continue to reside in Bikopur, while many branches of the family have migrated to different parts of India over time.
